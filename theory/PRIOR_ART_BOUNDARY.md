# Prior-Art Boundary

The following ideas are established neighbors and should not be presented as new by this project.

- Generalized tensor-product structures and minimal-scrambling subsystem selection.
- Mereological quantum phase transitions.
- Quantum-error-correction code switching and switching-cost optimization.
- Workload-aware qubit partitioning, placement, routing, and scheduling.
- Adaptive, neuromorphic, and plastic quantum hardware.
- Generic optimization with service/task costs plus switching costs.

## Current boundary

The project studies the combined question:

> Which quantum subsystem/factorization minimizes total cost when **task execution pressure** competes directly with **quantum scrambling/physical naturalness**, implementation error, and refactorization cost?

Candidate novelty must come from the joint objective, validated consequences, and experimentally or computationally supported regimes—not from alternative factorization, switching, adaptive couplings, or workload placement alone.

Use conservative language such as `candidate framework`, `workload–mereology competition`, `critical workload threshold`, and `reproducible benchmark`. Avoid claims of new quantum mechanics, beyond-BQP computation, quantum supremacy, or proven quantum speedup.
