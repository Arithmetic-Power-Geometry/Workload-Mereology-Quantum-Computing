# Changelog

## v1.0.0 — 2026-09-29

Initial research software release supporting workload–mereology quantum computing.

### Theory

- Dimensionless competition between normalized OQM scrambling cost and normalized task cost.
- Lower-convex-envelope characterization of workload-dependent optimal factorizations.
- Strict-compromise interval criterion for intermediate lower-hull vertices.
- Analytic four-qubit transition construction.

### Computational validation

- Exhaustive balanced-bipartition experiments.
- Operational Quantum Mereology Gaussian-scrambling controls.
- Pauli-ZZ experiments across uniform, normal, and lognormal coupling distributions.
- Generic noncommuting anisotropic XX+YY+ZZ Hamiltonian experiments.
- Fixed-seed finite-size studies through n = 10.
- Wilson confidence intervals for headline binomial proportions.

### Reproducibility

- Automated test and experiment workflow.
- Raw CSV and JSON numerical outputs.
- Statistical summaries.
- Figure-generation routines.
- Apache License 2.0.
